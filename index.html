Why Students Choose Us

Our curriculums are specifically designed to cater for the all-round development of the child. We combine the Montessori method of learning with other effective learning methods.

Importantly, we focus on the three domains of education built on the solid foundation of Gods word. These three domains of education are:

Cognitive Domain

Intellectual development and knowledge acquisition.

Affective Domain

Emotional growth, values, and spiritual maturity.

Psychomotor Domain

Physical skills and coordination through active learning.
