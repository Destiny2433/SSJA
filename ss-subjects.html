Senior Secondary Subjects

Our comprehensive WAEC and NECO aligned curriculum prepares students for future success in Science, Arts, and Commercial disciplines.

Core Compulsory Subjects

Regardless of the department (Science, Arts, or Commercial), all Senior Secondary students must offer the following core subjects:

English Language

General Mathematics

Civic Education

One Trade Subject

Departmental Subject Combinations

Science Stream

  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Further Mathematics
  • Agricultural Science
  • Technical Drawing
  • Geography

Arts / Humanities Stream

  • Literature in English
  • Government or History
  • CRS / IRS
  • Nigerian Language (Hausa/Igbo/Yoruba)
  • French
  • Visual Art

Commercial / Business Stream

  • Economics
  • Financial Accounting
  • Commerce
  • Government
  • Office Practice
  • Bookkeeping

Note: Students are expected to register for a minimum of eight (8) and a maximum of nine (9) subjects for their WAEC and NECO examinations.
